Arrow keys move and turn while exploring. M opens the map; M or Escape closes it. Click a party member to inspect their portrait and statistics. In combat, click a character to select them. Green cells show where they can move; click one to walk there. Red frames mark enemies you can attack by clicking. Choose a spell, then click a target with a cyan frame to cast it. Selecting another hero preserves each hero's remaining movement and action. Arrow keys also move the selected hero. Drag the map to pan; right-drag or Shift-drag rotates it. The mouse wheel zooms. Q and E rotate, plus and minus zoom, and Home restores the view. Combat commands use numbers 1 through 8; 0 returns to the previous menu. Page Up and Page Down turn command pages. D turns character detail pages.
