Arrow keys move and turn while exploring. M opens the map; M or Escape closes it. Click a party member to inspect their portrait and statistics. Combat commands use numbers 1 through 8; 0 returns to the previous menu. Choose Move, then use the arrow keys or click a reachable dungeon cell. Enter finishes movement. Choose an attack or spell, then click a highlighted target or use the cursor and Enter. Page Up and Page Down turn command pages. D turns character detail pages.
